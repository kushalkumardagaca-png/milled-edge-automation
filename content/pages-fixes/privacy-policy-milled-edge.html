Information collected

Privacy in practice

This page explains the information involved in using the site and the choices available to you.

This policy covers information you send, ordinary Blogger hosting logs, consent-based analytics and technical requests made by some tools. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser; optional analytics waits for your consent.

The sections below explain what each category is used for, who may process it and how long it may be retained.

— Kushal Kumar Daga Why it is built this way

There is no reading account or contact form. Information reaches the site through your email, ordinary Blogger logs, consent-based analytics or tool-provider requests described below.

  • What you give: your address and the text of your message, if you write to the desk — a name only if you type one.
  • What Blogger logs: the standard server line items — IP address, browser and platform, timestamps, the pages requested.
  • What a tool page may request: a country default, from an address lookup, so a calculator can start with your currency rather than someone else's.
  • What the publisher does not create: an advertising profile or purchase history. Blogger logs and consented analytics may still process technical and usage data described below.
Calculator arithmetic stays in your browser. The personal and corporate calculators compute locally; entering a salary into one does not send that salary anywhere. A copy or share action sends data only where you paste it.

04Analytics and advertising

Optional analytics

One optional service is in use: Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-DH0BYD3WZG. The GA4 tag loads only after you allow analytics; a saved choice is used on later visits. If analytics is declined or not yet allowed, this publication’s GA4 tag is not loaded. Blogger may still load its platform resources and process technical data as described in this policy.

  • With consent, the tag may send the page URL, referrer, approximate location inferred from IP, browser and device details, timestamps and a pseudonymous browser identifier to Google Analytics.
  • This tag is not configured to send email addresses, message contents or calculator inputs. URLs and referrers are sent, so do not place sensitive details in them.
  • Analytics retention follows the Google Analytics property setting. The publisher does not sell analytics data; Google processes it under its own service terms and property settings.

Your choice is remembered on your own device; the cookie settings control in the footer brings the panel back so you can change your mind at any time. Blocking cookies in your browser achieves the same result.

✦

✦The consent you keep

Consent choices

  1. The panel appearsThe panel offers “Allow analytics” or “Essential only”; GA4 remains off until you allow it.
  2. AllowThe GA4 tag loads and may process the page-view and device signals described above. The publisher uses aggregate reports; the tag may process pseudonymous events.
  3. Essential onlyNo GA4 script is loaded by this publication. Blogger platform resources may still load as needed.
  4. Change your mindThe footer control reopens the panel. Withdrawal prevents the tag loading on later page views; it does not erase data already sent, which remains subject to GA4 settings and applicable rights.

06Your rights

Your privacy rights

  • Access: ask what data connected to you exists.
  • Correct: fix anything inaccurate that you have shared.
  • Delete: ask for erasure, or remove your own comment under a page.
  • Object: decline analytics, or ask to be left out of correspondence, at any time.
  • Portability: ask for a copy of anything you gave, in a plain format.
  • Withdraw: pull back any consent you granted, with no explanation owed.

✦Where you live

Rights by jurisdiction

Choose where you are reading from; the answer changes, the address does not.

European Union, United Kingdom or Switzerland

Three lawful reasons cover the little data this site touches: your consent, for optional analytics; legitimate interest in keeping the site working and free of abuse; and legal duties where a record has to be kept. Consent can be withdrawn at any time from the consent panel, and withdrawing it does not undo what happened before.

If something here worries you, you may complain to your national data protection authority — the EU list is at edpb.europa.eu and the UK authority at ico.org.uk. For GDPR requests, a response is generally due without undue delay and within one month. Where a request is complex or numerous, the period may be extended by up to two further months; notice and reasons must be provided within the first month. Other laws may set different periods.

California and other US states with privacy laws

You may ask what personal information is held about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction and ask for deletion. Milled Edge does not sell personal information for money. Advertising personalisation is decided by the advertising provider, and no advertising network runs here today.

Where your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal and a provider honours it, that choice is respected. Where the CCPA applies, a verified request is generally answered within 45 calendar days; a single extension of up to another 45 days may be available where allowed, with notice. Other state laws may set different periods. Send requests to the same inbox.

Anywhere else

The same six rights are offered to every reader, whatever their local law requires: access, correction, deletion, objection, portability and withdrawal. Local law may give you more, and where it does, it applies in addition to this policy rather than instead of it.

Requests go to the approved inbox and are handled under the law that applies. If your concern is about data held by a provider rather than this publication, contact that provider directly.
