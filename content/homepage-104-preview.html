About the desk

Clear money writing, with the working shown.

MILLED EDGE is an independent financial-education publication written and published by Kushal Kumar Daga. Its coverage links household finance, business finance and market context. Sources and dates are checked, assumptions are made visible, and material errors can be reported for correction.

Educational material only—not personalised investment, legal or tax advice. Market-data timing varies by provider.
