Before you email

One clear contact route. Messages are reviewed by the publisher as capacity allows; a response is not guaranteed.

There is no contact form: your message goes to the approved inbox. Keep it concise, and do not include sensitive personal or financial information.

  • One topic per message. Please send one topic per email so it can be reviewed clearly.
  • No passwords, one-time codes, account numbers or identity documents. Never needed, and never asked for.
  • Corrections go first. If something on a page is wrong, say so plainly — accuracy outranks everything else here.

Share only what is needed

For a calculator or a business model, a small invented example is more useful than real records — and it is safer for you. Three habits make the difference:

  • Reproduce, do not disclose. Rounded or invented figures demonstrate almost any issue with a calculator.
  • Remove identifiers. Hide names, account numbers and balances from screenshots.
  • Use the right channel for urgency. Fraud, a stolen card or an account takeover belongs with your bank, broker or the authorities — not with a publication.
General questions and corrections. Use the inbox for publication questions and corrections. For advice about your circumstances, consult an appropriately qualified professional.

Choose the right desk

Six enquiry types, with a short checklist to help us locate the page, tool or policy you mean.

Published pages

The page title and exact address help us locate it.

  • The page address and the sentence concerned.
  • What you expected to read, and what it says.
  • A public source and its date, if you have one.

Privacy & site

A question about the privacy policy, consent choices, site access or a correction to site information.

  • The page address and the relevant section.
  • The choice or explanation you are asking about.
  • Do not include passwords, account details or sensitive records.

Calculators

A result that looks wrong, or a field that will not behave.

  • The calculator's name, and the country or currency you used.
  • Invented or rounded sample figures — never your real records.
  • What you expected, what appeared, and which browser you used.

Corporate

A model, a template or a planning assumption.

  • Which model: NPV, runway, valuation or something else.
  • Non-confidential sample figures.
  • The assumption you think is doing too much work.

Money Atlas

A country figure, a comparison or an eligibility rule.

  • The country, the metric and the value shown.
  • An official source and the date it applies from.
  • Whether you are looking at a manual reference figure or a live quote.

Markets

A price, a chart, a symbol or a NAV.

  • The instrument, the exchange and the currency.
  • The source timestamp, and when you checked.
  • A note if the price differs from your broker's — theirs is the one that trades.

Frequently asked questions

Twelve concise answers to help before you write.

Can you review my portfolio or tell me what to do with my money?
No. This publication is education, not personal advice, and it does not manage money for readers. What a question here can do is become a page that explains the principle behind it. For a decision about your own file, the right call is a registered investment adviser who can see your whole position and is accountable for the answer.
Will you cover a specific topic?
Reader suggestions inform future coverage alongside research priorities. No page or publication date is promised. Send a specific topic or question; do not include personal financial records.
I found an error on a page.
Thank you — genuinely. Send the link and what looks wrong. Where useful, a correction is noted on the page with its review date; there is no promised correction time.
Can I translate or republish a page?
Short quotations with attribution and a link are welcome. Full-page copying, translations and mirrors need written permission; figures and meaning must not be altered.
How soon will I actually get a reply?
Messages are reviewed as capacity allows; no response time is guaranteed. Corrections with a checkable source receive priority.
Do you accept guest posts or paid placements?
No guest posts, sponsored content, paid reviews or placements are accepted.
Is Milled Edge a registered investment adviser or research analyst?
No. Milled Edge is an educational publication. It is not a registered investment adviser, not a research analyst and not a portfolio manager, and it does not issue recommendations, target prices or buy-or-sell calls. Nothing published here is personalised advice; a decision about your own money belongs with a professional who is registered, can see your full position and is accountable for the answer.
Can I reuse a calculator figure in my own work?
Yes, with attribution and a link back to the page. Every calculator output is an illustration built on the assumptions printed beside it, so quote the assumptions with the number. Do not present a modelled figure as a forecast, a guarantee or a recommendation.
Why do two calculators give different answers for the same loan?
Usually a different assumption, not a different sum: reducing balance versus flat rate, whether processing fees are financed, how often interest compounds, and whether a prepayment is applied at the start or the end of a year. Check those four before deciding one tool is wrong.
Do I need an account, and is what I type stored?
No account and no login anywhere on the site. Calculator maths runs inside your own browser; the figures you enter are never sent to Milled Edge and are never stored. Analytics is off until a reader allows it, and the consent choice is kept in your own browser.
A calculator or page will not load. What do you need from me?
Send the page link, the device and browser you are using, and what you expected to happen. A screenshot helps if it carries no personal or financial details. Reports are reviewed as capacity allows, and reproducible breakage is fixed ahead of new material.
Do you have a newsletter, WhatsApp or Telegram channel?
Not currently. There is no newsletter service, no broadcast list and no messaging channel. Corrections and updates appear on the page itself with a review date. Any account claiming to be Milled Edge on another channel should be treated as unverified.
