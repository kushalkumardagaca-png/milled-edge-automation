02 / Optional measurement

Analytics waits for permission.

Measurement is optional. The rest of the site remains available if you decline it.

Google Analytics 4

The site uses measurement ID G-DH0BYD3WZG. The analytics script loads only if you choose “Allow analytics” in the consent panel. If you choose “Essential only,” the analytics script is not loaded.

You can revisit the consent control in the site footer. Analytics retention follows the setting configured in the GA4 property; Google processes data under its own terms.

One simple rule

No optional analytics before your choice. Declining it does not block calculators or pages.

03 / Other services

Some requests go beyond this site.

External providers have their own policies and controls.

Blogger, Google and email

Google hosts this website on Blogger. Email sent to the publisher is received through Gmail. These services may process technical or message data under their own privacy notices. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Charts, market data and other embedded tools

Some pages request charts, prices, currency or other data from external providers. When you use those features, your browser may send a standard request to the provider named or linked on that page. The provider’s own privacy terms apply to that request.

Cookies and browser storage

Blogger may use essential cookies to operate and secure the site. The consent control stores your analytics choice in browser storage; optional analytics may use its own storage only when you allow it. You can also manage cookies in your browser settings.

Retention and deletion

Email is kept only as needed to handle the correspondence. Comments can be removed through Blogger controls or by request. Google manages hosting logs, and analytics retention follows the GA4 property setting. No specific retention period is promised here for provider-managed records.
