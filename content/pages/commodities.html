Price basis and limitations

Monthly physical-commodity benchmark averages; not live quotes or retail prices. Each series has its own grade, geography and delivery basis. Explicit futures/forward-month series, commodity indexes, and financial securities are excluded. These are source-specific grade, location, import/export, auction, fixing, spot, or physical-settlement price series—not one universal price for every physical delivery. Gold and silver figures are international benchmarks, not Indian jewellery-shop or retail cash prices; purity, duties, taxes and local premiums are not included.

Source: World Bank Commodity Price Data (The Pink Sheet) — November 2026 sheet (PDF) · monthly source workbook.

Only commodity price series with a reported October 2026 value and a non-futures physical-market basis are listed. Explicit futures/forward-month quotations, commodity indexes, ETFs, company shares, bonds and other financial instruments are omitted.
