Price basis and limitations

Monthly physical-commodity benchmark averages; not live quotes or retail prices. Each series has its own grade, geography and delivery basis. Explicit futures/forward-month series, broad aggregate commodity indexes, and financial securities are excluded. These are source-specific grade, location, import/export, auction, fixing, spot, or physical-settlement price series—not one universal price for every physical delivery. Gold and silver figures are international benchmarks, not Indian jewellery-shop or retail cash prices; purity, duties, taxes and local premiums are not included.

Source: World Bank Commodity Price Data (The Pink Sheet) — November 2026 sheet (PDF) · monthly source workbook.

Only commodity price series with a reported October 2026 value and a non-futures physical-market basis are listed. Explicit futures/forward-month quotations, aggregate commodity indexes, ETFs, company shares, bonds and other financial instruments are omitted. The source does not assign exchange tickers to these physical price series; no symbols have been invented.

Commodity price questions

Are these live or intraday prices?

No. They are the latest reported monthly averages for October 2026, released on November 3, 2026.

Are gold and silver shown at local retail or jewellery rates?

No. They are international reference benchmarks; local taxes, purity, import duties, dealer premiums and making charges are not included.

Are futures or financial products included?

No. Explicit futures and forward-month quotations and financial securities are excluded. Each listed row is a physical-commodity price series with its stated location, grade and delivery basis.

Why does each commodity show a different location?

Physical commodities do not have one universal cash price. The source reports specific markets, qualities and delivery bases, shown on each entry.
